## Researching ideas. Engineering systems.

**VoidStack Labs** is an independent learning and engineering space focused on understanding intelligent systems and turning technical ideas into working software.

I explore machine learning, deep learning, data science, and AI-powered applications through hands-on experiments, implementation, and iterative development. The aim is to understand not only how a model or system works, but also the decisions and trade-offs behind it.

## Research & engineering interests

- **Machine learning:** classification, regression, feature engineering, model evaluation, and optimization
- **Deep learning:** neural networks, training workflows, and framework experimentation
- **AI systems:** LLM-powered applications and conversational systems
- **Data science:** data analysis, visualization, and statistical reasoning
- **Software engineering:** Python applications, APIs, and full-stack development
